# 034 — Transmission

Goal: broadcast one typographic instruction to three independent receivers.
Candidates: BroadcastChannel transmission wall; offscreen-worker ink;
CSS-anchored annotation. Selected channel delivery for unused messaging semantics;
unlike 029's DOM mutation observation, receivers consume structured-cloned data.
Mechanism Signature: structured record -> three channel events -> split bold text.
Design Signature: sans; orange/green/purple; triptych; posters; abrupt transmission.

A randomly named same-origin channel isolates the demo. Three separate channel
objects own their panels. Trusted input posts one message; each callback validates
its payload, records delivery and changes weight/word spacing. pagehide closes
objects. No cross-tab or network behavior is claimed; all receivers are in this
document. Initial visibility failure exposed missing text-node spacing between
block spans; a literal space restored the semantic phrase. Second verify OK.
Opened primary and interaction captures: all three phrases are legible and
their weight/word separation visibly changes together without clipping.
Acceptance requires
three distinct delivered IDs and three changed panels, not just send completion.
