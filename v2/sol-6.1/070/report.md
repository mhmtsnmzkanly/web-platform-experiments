Candidates: validity gate; color-space ink; declarative motion. Selected

Design Signature: sans; blue/violet/lavender; proof line; validation mark; reject.

patternMismatch and one invalid event; :invalid styles the dominant heading.
Evidence checks actual native state, unchanged value and false acceptance.

as single-line blue/violet sans. Corrected before/after images opened: clear heavy
tracked rejection mark and accurate status, with unchanged readable subject.

of greetings; zero external resources or permissions.
