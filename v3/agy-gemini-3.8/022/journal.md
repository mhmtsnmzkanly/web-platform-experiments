  - Typographic Behavior: The closed loops of 'O' produce stable beehives or blinkers; the high-density intersection of 'H' and 'R' triggers chaotic glider cascades; the isolated stems of 'L' either collapse into blocks or spark localized oscillators.
- **Causal Role of "HELLO WORLD"**:
  - The text is the literal physical DNA of the universe. Different strings ("HELLO WORLD" vs any other text) yield completely different glider trajectories, population survival curves, and asymptotic attractors.
- **Visual Aesthetic**:
  - Cyanotype blueprint drafting table (`#0a1d37`, `#133b5c`, `#1e5f74`, `#e0f2fe`), crisp drafting millimeter grids, architectural technical notations, white ink cells, and a real-time population seismograph.
- **Novelty & Moving Frontier**:
