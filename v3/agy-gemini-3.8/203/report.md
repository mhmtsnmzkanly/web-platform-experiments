# Experiment Report: 203 — CHAMBRE NOIRE D'ATELIER N° 203 // Grand Format 8×10 Pouces

## Frontier Classification: 19th-Century Large-Format Field Camera Optics, 3D Accordion Bellows Kinematics & Pure CSS Photochemistry State Machine

Experiment 203 establishes a pure CSS frontier in **optical physics, mechanical camera engineering, and historical photochemistry**:
1. **Large-Format 8×10 Camera Obscura Craftsmanship**:
   - Conceived as a premier 1888 French studio view camera crafted by master cabinetmakers and opticians of the *Manufacture d'Instruments d'Optique (Paris)*.
   - Built around an authentic material palette: Cuban mahogany wood grain (`#241209`, `#421e10`, `#612c17`), mirror-polished brass hardware (`#fff0a8`, `#f7d868`, `#b08520`), pleated bellows leather (`#151110`, `#38241e`), optical flint glass (`rgba(185, 225, 245, 0.9)`), and frosted ground glass (`rgba(238, 242, 243, 0.9)`).
   - **Zero Faux Digital Overlays**: Strictly rejects digital screens, sci-fi HUDs, flat UI dashboards, and retro-futuristic displays. Relies entirely on mechanical brass rack-and-pinion rails, knurled thumbscrews, liquid spirit levels, leather accordion folds, and pneumatic rubber squeeze bulbs.
2. **100% Pure HTML & CSS Runtime (Zero JavaScript)**:
   - Contains **zero scripts** (no `<script>`, inline event handlers, `javascript:` URLs, external assets, or JS test hooks).
   - Powered entirely by semantic HTML5 inputs (`radio`, `checkbox`), adjacent sibling selectors, and CSS `:has()` relational selectors.
3. **Optical Mechanisms & Spatial Components**:
   - **3D Articulated Accordion Bellows**: Realistic triple-extension pleated leather bellows utilizing CSS 3D perspective, directional light-and-shadow linear gradients, and dynamic elongation/compression.
   - **Camera Obscura Ground Glass Projection (8×10)**: Faithful optical physics simulation where the projected atelier still life is naturally **inverted (upside down) and laterally reversed** (`transform: scale(-1, -1)`).
   - **Intricate Parisian Atelier Still Life**: Features a sculpted classical marble Apollo bust, a brass celestial globe with tilted axis and meridian rings, and an open leatherbound manuscript displaying calligraphic typography: *"HELLO WORLD • ATELIER PARIS 1888"*.
   - **Petzval Portrait Lens & 12-Blade Iris Diaphragm**: Concentric brass stepped barrels, Waterhouse stop slot, optical reflections, and dynamic iris aperture hole scaling from wide open f/2.8 down to f/64 pinhole.
   - **Dynamic Optical Bokeh & Depth of Field**: Wide apertures generate creamy circular bokeh orbs with luminous rim rings and shallow focal isolation; stopping down expands depth-of-field to infinite sharpness.
   - **Rack-and-Pinion Focusing Rail**: Sliding the geared brass rail shifts the sharp plane of focus between foreground manuscript, midground marble bust, and background celestial globe.
   - **Scheimpflug Tilt & Shift Kinematics**: Tilts the front lens standard forward (+12°) or laterally (+14°), rotating the Scheimpflug focal plane across the floor or diagonal plane.
   - **Pneumatic Shutter Release Bulb (*Déclencheur à Poire*)**: Tactile vulcanized rubber squeeze bulb connected via braided silk tubing; squeezing the bulb snaps the leaf shutter curtain.
   - **Miniature Brass Spirit Level**: Functional green spirit level vial with floating glass bubble mounted on the rear standard frame.
4. **Historical Photochemistry & Inspection Suite**:
   - **Collodion Humide (1851)**: Silver halide wet-plate tintype with deep sepia-charcoal shadows, delicate silver mid-tones, and authentic plate luster.
   - **Cyanotype Bleu de Prusse (1842)**: Iron salt process with rich Prussian blue shadows (`#0b233a`) and crisp unexposed parchment highlights.
   - **Platinotype Neutre (1873)**: Archival velvety platinum-gray gradation with neutral tonal fidelity.
   - **Autochrome Lumière (1907)**: The world's first color plate process utilizing a dyed microscopic potato starch filter matrix, producing an Impressionist pointillist chromatic palette.
   - **4× Optical Focusing Loupe**: Brass-rimmed photographer's loupe magnifying the ground-glass grain and focal plane subject details.
   - **Dark Velvet Focusing Cloth (*Voile de Mise au Point*)**: Drapes over the rear camera standard, plunging the perimeter into velvet black and maximizing viewing contrast.
   - **Red Darkroom Safelight Mode (*Lanterne Inactinique*)**: 630nm deep ruby red safelight illumination across the atelier workshop bench.
