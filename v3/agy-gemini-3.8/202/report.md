# Experiment Report: 202 — CHRONOMÈTRE MÉCANIQUE N° 202 // Grand Complication Astronomique

## Frontier Classification: Haute Horlogerie Astronomical Grand Complication & Pure CSS Mechanical Engineering Architecture

Experiment 202 establishes a pure CSS frontier in **mechanical horology and spatial micro-engineering**:
1. **Haute Horlogerie & Astronomical Micro-Mechanics**:
   - Conceived as an ultra-luxury pocket-watch / astronomical grand complication executed by master artisans of the *Manufacture d'Horlogerie d'Art (Genève)*.
   - Built around an authentic watchmaking palette: hand-finished 18K rose gold (`#c89666`, `#8e5932`), 950 platinum (`#d8dadc`, `#84888e`), Grade 5 blue titanium (`#2c5270`), flame-blued Breguet steel (`#18457e`, `#2463b2`), synthetic corundum rubies (`#d91b5c`), and dial silvering (`#ece9e2`, `#f6f4ee`).
   - **Zero Faux Digital Overlays**: Strictly rejects digital screens, sci-fi HUDs, CRT effects, retro-futuristic monitors, and flat UI conventions. Relies entirely on procedural guilloché engine-turning, circular graining (*perlage*), Geneva stripes (*Côtes de Genève*), mirror-polished chamfers (*anglage*), and blued screws.
2. **100% Pure HTML & CSS Runtime (Zero JavaScript)**:
   - Contains **zero scripts** (no `<script>`, inline event handlers, `javascript:` URLs, external scripts, or JS test hooks).
   - Entirely powered by semantic HTML5 inputs (`radio`, `checkbox`), CSS sibling selectors, and CSS `:has()` relational pseudo-classes.
3. **Mechanical Complications & Micro-Components**:
   - **60-Second Flying Tourbillon Regulator**: Open-heart aperture at 6 o'clock with mirror-polished bridge, 3-arm titanium carriage rotating at 1 RPM, balance wheel oscillating at 3 Hz (21,600 vibrations per hour / 6 beats per second), balance regulating screws, and central synthetic ruby capstone.
   - **Astronomical Moonphase Display**: Precision semicircular aperture at 12 o'clock with deep lapis-lazuli starfield and dynamic waxing/waning lunar disk.
   - **72-Hour Power Reserve Indicator**: Sector gauge at 9 o'clock with blued sector hand indicating remaining barrel spring torque.
   - **30-Minute Chronograph Totalizer**: Concentric snailed (*azuré*) subsidiary register at 3 o'clock with blued recorder needle.
   - **Breguet Hands**: Traditional flame-blued steel *pomme évidée* hands with counterweights and fine sweep seconds hand.
   - **Guilloché Dial**: Intricate sunburst barleycorn engine-turning rendered via procedural `repeating-conic-gradient` and radial gradient masks.
   - **3D Exhibition Sapphire Caseback**: Perspective flip (`perspective: 1400px`, `transform-style: preserve-3d`, `rotateY(180deg)`) revealing the internal Calibre 202: 45° Côtes de Genève striping, perlage baseplate, 21K gold skeletonized oscillating rotor with tungsten rim, column-wheel chronograph levers, and gold chatons.

4. **Multi-State Mechanical Control Suite**:
   - **Spatial Perspective Flip**: Switches between the Guilloché Dial face and the 3D Sapphire Caseback.
   - **3-Position Crown & Stem**:
     - *Position 0 (Remontage)*: Standard winding position (full 72h reserve).
     - *Position 1 (Phase Lunaire)*: Moonphase rapid correction gear engagement.
     - *Position 2 (Mise à l'Heure)*: Balance hacking mechanism and hour/minute time setting.
   - **Case Alloy & Finishing Matrix**:
     - *Or Rose 18K & Argenté (AU)*: Warm 5N rose gold bezel, brushed silver dial, blued steel hands.
     - *Platine 950 & Ardoise (PT)*: Cool platinum 950 case, slate anthracite dial, rhodium indices.
     - *Titane Bleu Grade 5 (TI)*: Anodized midnight blue titanium case and avant-garde bridges.
   - **Horological Loupe (2.5×)**: Optical magnification inspection focusing on the tourbillon escapement, hairspring, and jewel pivots.
   - **UV Inspection Darkroom (Super-LumiNova)**: Simulates 365nm ultraviolet testing, illuminating phosphorescent emerald indices, hands, and tourbillon markers.
   - **Monopusher Chronograph**: Realizes start, stop, and flyback reset states via pure CSS animation control.
