# Experiment 113 Journal: Responsive / Environmental Frontier

## Frontier Assessment: Responsive / Environmental Frontier
The Responsive / Environmental Frontier requires that the experiment meaningfully react to display dimensions, container bounds, or environmental context.
"Do not reduce this to: simply scaling font size with a media query. The environmental condition must restructure the mechanism itself."

## Candidate Formulations

### Candidate A: Adaptive Topological Manifold & CSS Container-Query Metrology Workstation
- **Concept**: A responsive typographic chamber housing the 10 characters ('H', 'E', 'L', 'L', 'O', 'W', 'O', 'R', 'L', 'D') whose governing mechanical physics and topological graph structure mutate dynamically across container dimension tiers (powered by `@container (inline-size > ...)` and `ResizeObserver`):
  1. **Ultrawide Linear Ribbon ($w \ge 820\text{px}$)**: Continuous 1D wave dispersion network with elastic tensile coupling between adjacent glyphs ($v_p = \sqrt{T/\mu}$).
  2. **Bilateral Bilayer Split ($560\text{px} \le w < 820\text{px}$)**: Word breaks into two grammatical clusters ("HELLO" and "WORLD"), coupling as a dual-tier antiphonal coupled harmonic oscillator.
  3. **Cartesian Cellular Matrix ($380\text{px} \le w < 560\text{px}$)**: Restructures into a $2 \times 5$ contact-compression grid with hydrostatic pressure redistribution.
  4. **Columnar Monolith ($w < 380\text{px}$)**: Restructures into a vertical gravity compression column where compressive normal stress accumulates monotonically down the 10 letter blocks: $\sigma_k = \sum_{j=1}^k m_j g$.
- **Causal Hello World Integration**: The 10 characters are the discrete physical bodies of the manifold. Their spatial adjacency, spring constants, coupled harmonic modes, and compressive stresses change categorically as the container resizes.
- **Evidence Strategy**: Container width ($w$), current active mechanical regime (1..4), topological edge count, accumulated compressive stress, and verified transition upon dimension change.

### Candidate B: Device Pixel Ratio (DPR) Subpixel Antialiasing Profiler
- **Concept**: Rendering varying subpixel glyph meshes depending on window.devicePixelRatio.
- **Trade-off**: Hard to exercise dynamically in automated headless testing without DevTools emulation commands.

### Candidate C: Multi-Column Typographic Fragmentation Engine
- **Concept**: CSS column layout fracturing "HELLO WORLD" syllables across columns.
- **Trade-off**: Less rich physical kinematics than the 4-regime topological manifold.

## Selection
**Candidate A** is chosen. It demonstrates:
1. Native Web Platform `@container` queries and continuous container manipulation.
2. Complete structural restructuring of the physical mechanism (1D Wave $\to$ Coupled Bilayer $\to$ 2D Matrix $\to$ 1D Vertical Column).
3. Direct causal Hello World role: The 10 letters form the bodies whose physical equations of motion are rewritten by the container geometry.
