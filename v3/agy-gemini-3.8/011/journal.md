# Experiment 011 — Research & Candidate Selection

## Context & Frontier Objectives
Following the culmination of the initial 001–010 sequence, Experiment 011 initiates the second frontier exploration (011–020).
In line with the V3 design mandate and direct user guidance, 011 consciously breaks away from:
1. Multi-system kitchen-sink compounding (avoiding unnecessary subsystem stacking).
2. The particle swarm / force-field kinetic reflex (seen in 002, 004, and 010).
3. The dark obsidian telemetry HUD aesthetic (which risks creating a repetitive laboratory house style).
4. Linear horizontal letter baseline arrangements.

The objective of 011 is to open a new **Computational Growth & Morphogenetic Grammar Frontier**: formal language theory, string rewriting systems (L-Systems), and fractal turtle kinematics to generate an organic, growing specimen whose anatomical branches and botanical foliage naturally unfurl the phrase "HELLO WORLD".

---

## Candidate 1: Lindenmayer Formal Grammar (L-System) & Botanical Herbarium Morphogenesis
- **Concept**: A formal deterministic/stochastic Lindenmayer string rewriting engine ($G = (V, \omega, P)$). The axiom $\omega$ and production rules $P$ are synthesized directly from the structural character topology of "HELLO WORLD". An animated turtle geometry interpreter renders recursive branch bifurcations, leaf phyllotaxis, and flower blossoms whose apical buds expand and blossom into the 10 glyphs of "HELLO WORLD".
- **Strengths**:
  - Genuinely new mechanism family (formal grammar theory, recursive string rewriting, botanical phyllotaxis).
  - Radical visual departure: Japanese herbarium specimen / pressed botanical engraving on aged warm washi paper (`#fbf8ee`), forest moss greens (`#1c3829`, `#3e6b48`), and cinnabar red accents (`#c2410c`).
  - Completely eliminates particle swarms, physics grids, and dark HUDs.
  - Clean, focused web platform surface: SVG 2.0 parametric recursive paths + CSS custom properties + clean semantic DOM.
- **Weaknesses**: Must ensure the turtle recursion finishes within frame budgets without deep call-stack recursion overflow (can be iterative using state stack `[x, y, angle]`).

## Candidate 2: Margolus-Block Reversible Cellular Automata Lattice
- **Concept**: A 2D partitioning cellular automaton using Margolus $2 \times 2$ blocks to simulate reversible billiard-ball typography or sand-grain avalanches forming "HELLO WORLD".
- **Strengths**: Pure discrete computation; reversible micro-physics.
- **Weaknesses**: Shares visual traits with 003's 2D grid lattice; might feel like a pixel-art toy if not handled with immense visual elegance.

## Candidate 3: Huygens-Fresnel Optical Wavefront & Coherent Aperture Diffraction
- **Concept**: Coherent monochromatic wave simulation propagating through 10 slit apertures corresponding to "HELLO WORLD", rendering near-field Fresnel and far-field Fraunhofer diffraction fringe patterns.
- **Strengths**: Rich optical physics.
- **Weaknesses**: Heavy 2D numerical convolution on CPU typed arrays, reminiscent of 006's distance fields or 002's Chladni waves.

## Selection & Architectural Specification: Candidate 1 (L-System Botanical Herbarium)
- **Selected**: Candidate 1.
- **Mechanism Depth**:
  - Formal alphabet: $V = \{F, +, -, [, ], A, B, \dots\}$.
  - Axiom $\omega = \text{"X"}$.
  - Production rules derived from character glyph features:
    - $X \to F[+X][-X]FX$
    - $F \to FF$
  - Turtle state stack: $[(x, y, \theta, \text{depth})]$.
  - Apical meristem bud maturation: as branch growth reaches terminal buds, golden ratio Fibonacci phyllotaxis ($\phi \approx 137.5^\circ$) arranges the petals and foliage into the ten glyph letters of "HELLO WORLD".
  - Environmental wind sway: continuous gentle harmonic oscillation ($A \sin(\omega t + \phi)$) applied through branch joint angles.
- **Visual Design**:
  - Antique Japanese herbarium specimen / botanical lithograph on textured warm washi cream paper (`#f8f5eb`).
  - Delicate forest pine and moss inks (`#1a3323`, `#2d5a3e`), earthy umber twigs (`#5c4033`), and vermilion seal stamp accent (`#b91c1c`).
  - Clear vertical-horizontal asymmetrical specimen layout with botanical taxonomic classification typography.
- **Evidence Contract**:
  - `window.labEvidence` measures:
    - `grammarProductionRulesCount`: 4 rules.
    - `grammarDerivationDepth`: 4 generations.
    - `totalBotanicalBranches`: count of rendered recursive branch segments.
    - `apicalMeristemGlyphs`: 10 terminal letters of "HELLO WORLD".
    - `meanBranchTensionAngle`: dynamic wind sway angle.
