# Experiment Report: 204 — OPUS OPTICUM // Pavillon d'Art Cinétique & Typographie Gestalt

## Frontier Classification: Kinetic Op-Art, Gestalt Perceptual Psychology & Abstract Typographic Architecture

Experiment 204 marks a **fundamental paradigm shift** in the repository's creative and architectural lineage:
1. **Radical Departure from the Skeuomorphic Hardware Formula**:
   - Strictly abandons the physical device / instrument / chassis pattern of experiments 201–203 (no terminals, no pocket watches, no bellows cameras).
   - Eliminates symmetrical flank instrument dashboards, technical spec plaques, brass hardware, and wood cabinetry.
   - Migrates entirely into **contemporary fine art, optical physics, human visual cortex perception, and Gestalt psychology** inspired by Bridget Riley, Victor Vasarely, and Josef Albers.
2. **100% Pure HTML5 & CSS3 Runtime (Zero JavaScript)**:
   - Contains **zero scripts** (no `<script>`, inline event handlers, `javascript:` URLs, external assets, or JS test hooks).
   - All perceptual phenomena, grating frequencies, kinetic tempos, and gallery illumination modes are managed via semantic `<input type="radio">` controls and CSS `:has()` parent-state selectors.
3. **Five Monumental Perceptual Phenomena**:
   - **01 Moiré Cinétique (Moiré Wave Interference)**: Superimposes a conical radial grating rotating clockwise against a concentric radial grating rotating counter-clockwise under `mix-blend-mode: difference`. Produces true physiological wave interference fringes rippling across the visual field with "HELLO WORLD" emerging in inverse phase.
   - **02 Sphère Vasarely (3D Geodesic Bulge)**: Transforms the square canvas into a circular convex lens with radial perspective distortion, thrusting the typographic core forward in a hyper-dimensional spherical protrusion.
   - **03 Contours Kanizsa (Gestalt Illusory Contours)**: Inspired by Gaetano Kanizsa's triangle illusion. "HELLO WORLD" is stripped of solid fills and stroke borders; its contours emerge in the viewer's visual cortex as emergent Gestalt boundaries generated by surrounding pac-man discs and radiant vectors.
   - **04 Shimmer Chromatique (Retinal Opponent-Process Vibration)**: Exploits chromatic opponent-processing by colliding hyper-saturated electric vermilion (`#ff2244`) against vivid cyan (`#00f0ff`) with yellow secondary highlights, generating intense retinal afterimages and physiological shimmer.
   - **05 Vortex Anamorphique (Anamorphic Logarithmic Funnel)**: Conical perspective tunnel with pulsating radial masks pulling "HELLO WORLD" into an infinite optical vortex.
4. **Secondary Curatorial Axes**:
   - **Grating Frequency**: Switchable between Low Frequency (12px structural stripes), Medium Frequency (6px moiré waves), and Ultra-Dense (3px high-frequency retinal vibration).
   - **Kinetic Tempo**: Hypnotique Rapide (8s period), Dérive Méditative (22s slow ambient drift), and Stase Absolue (static frozen tension).
   - **Museum Gallery Illumination**: Chambre Noire (deep obsidian gallery), Galerie Blanche (high-key White Cube museum daylight), and Néon Spectral (ultraviolet chromatic radiance).
